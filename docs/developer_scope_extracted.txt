SCANWICK — DEVELOPER 
SCOPE FOR THE NEW PRD
What survives, what is corrected, what is deleted, what must be built.
Bank Statement analyzer is the product. Ecommerce ingestion survives only as the cash-verification input. Sales 
analyzer is cancelled.
Measured against the codebase on 27 July 2026.
1. THE DECISION, IN NUMBERS
Measured against the actual codebase on 27 July 2026.
SERVICES: 41 files, 6,124 lines are KEEPERS. 34 files, 4,906 lines are REMOVED. That is 44 percent of the 
service layer deleted.
TESTS: 19 sales-specific test files, 3,195 lines, deleted with them. The remaining suite is 105 files.
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PRD: you are not writing a new specification from zero. You are writing a 
specification for a system that already has a working ingestion layer, a working mapping layer, a correct net 
margin engine, a correct balance integrity check, and a reconciliation spine. The new PRD documents what 
survives, corrects what is wrong, and adds what is missing. It should be roughly half the length of the last one.
2. KEEP — THE FOUNDATION THE NEW PRD SITS ON
2.1 The three strategic assets — treat these as first-class in the PRD
reconciliation.py and the reconciliation_reports table. Every analysis writes a record: source file, date range, 
currency conversions, records excluded and why, disabled features, contextual markers applied. Every metric 
links back through analysis_run_id. THIS IS THE PRODUCT. Analysis is being commoditised; verifiable 
provenance is not. The new PRD should build outward from this, not treat it as compliance overhead.
column_mapping.py and column_mapping_store.py plus the column_mappings table. Four-tier resolution, exact 
then fuzzy then confirm then unmapped, with monetary fields blocked from fuzzy auto-apply. Saved per 
merchant on a header signature so repeat uploads are zero-touch. This is why Scanwick accepts files other tools 
reject.
mono_client.py and mono_ingestion.py. The zero-effort data path. Today it is the primary ingestion route; when 
the NIBSS registry goes live it becomes one adapter among two. Keep the abstraction clean so open banking 
slots in beside it.
2.2 Bank statement core — all keepers
bank_ingestion.py — CSV parsing and canonical row writing for all 13 bank formats plus generic fallback.
bank_pdf_ingestion.py — PDF and OCR path for scanned statements.
bank_account_integrity.py — balance integrity check at 0.01 tolerance, and interbank own-account transfer 
detection. Correct as built. Do not touch.
bank_transaction_classification.py — payment mode and category classification (transfer, POS, cash 
withdrawal, mobile money, direct debit, standing order, bank charge).
bank_cashflow.py — eligible_transactions, the single point that excludes is_anomalous and own-account 
transfers from every downstream calculation. Correct and important.
bank_dashboard.py — Tier 1 descriptive summary.

bank_income_stability.py — coefficient of variation and stability class.
bank_abm.py — average monthly balance, 3, 6 and 12 month windows.
bank_cashflow_analysis.py — cash buffer, expense concentration, recurring versus variable, business versus 
personal.
bank_fraud_risk.py — z-score, structuring, duplicate payee, timing anomalies, composite 0-100 score with 
explainable breakdown.
bank_loan_readiness.py — the 30/25/25/20 composite, creditworthiness tier, improvement recommendations, 
estimated debt coverage.
bank_lender_brief.py — keep the orchestration and the metric assembly. The rendering is rebuilt (see section 3).
bank_playbook.py — AI recommendations for the borrower-facing view.
bank_cashflow_forecast.py — 90-day projection and cash runway. Second-phase keeper; lenders care about 
runway.
2.3 Ecommerce — only what feeds cash verification
ecommerce_ingestion.py — sales record parsing. Needed because sales records are how you prove unbanked 
cash revenue.
ecommerce_margins.py — the net margin formula. Implemented exactly to the Developer Guide, correctly

ecommerce_revenue.py — revenue aggregation. Needed for the cash-gap comparison. Has a currency defect to 
fix.
ecommerce_order_items.py — line-item joins.
ecommerce_dashboard.py — minimal summary.
EVERYTHING ELSE IN ECOMMERCE IS DELETED. The ecommerce analyzer is no longer a product. It is 
one input to the bank product.

3. FIX — KEEPERS THAT ARE BROKEN
These are in the new PRD as corrections, not as new features. The behaviour they describe is already specified; 
the implementation is wrong.
bank_loan_readiness.py lines 59-68 — ABM averages only days that had transactions. Verified 64.9 percent 
understatement. Must carry the balance forward across zero-activity days. Line 72 — use calendar months, not 
months times 30.
bank_loan_readiness.py lines 265-275 — weights are silently renormalized when a component is unavailable, so 
the API reports 40 percent where the PRD fixes 30. Report true fixed weights plus a maximum achievable score.
bank_loan_readiness.py line 54 — a coefficient of variation of exactly 40 is classified volatile; the PRD places 
40 in moderate.
bank_lender_brief.py lines 20-27, 78, 92-118 — section keys do not match the specification, values are 
dictionaries where prose is required, and the renderer truncates at 110 characters on a single page with no 
wrapping. Rebuild the rendering and generate real narrative per section.

bank_fraud_risk.py — the z-score at 3.0 and round-number structuring detection will flag ajo, esusu and adashe 
as fraud. Add contributory-savings detection and report the pattern as a positive signal.
routes/bank.py lines 64-69 — a single READ_ROLES set gives loan officers full transaction detail. Split it. 
Your buyers are loan officers.
ecommerce_revenue.py lines 50-74 — sums mixed currencies and labels the total with the first row's currency. 
Use base_currency_amount.
ecommerce_ingestion.py lines 370-374 — rows without an order ID are never deduplicated, so re-upload 
doubles revenue. Deterministic content hash plus a unique constraint.
ingestion, all analyzers — dates parse month-first. Add dayfirst handling and read date_locale from stored 
value_rules. Flag ambiguous dates rather than guessing.
column_mapping.py synonym dictionary — 'total cost' resolves to unit_cogs at exact match, treating a line total 
as a per-unit cost.
quality reporting — rejected rows currently emit no warnings at all. Every rejection must produce a named 
warning. This is the PRD's founding principle and it is not implemented on the rejection path.
rbac.py and every route — merchant_id arrives as a client-supplied query parameter and is computable from 
user_id. Derive from session; enforce as a router dependency rather than inside handler bodies.
4. DELETE — AND WHY
4.1 Sales analyzer — 17 services, 3,195 lines of tests, all models and 
routes
Delete: sales_deals, sales_forecast, sales_ingestion, sales_notifications, sales_pipeline, sales_playbook, 
sales_postmortem, sales_quality, sales_rbac_scoping, sales_rep_leaderboard, sales_rep_trajectory, 
sales_slippage, sales_stage_timing, sales_stage_velocity, sales_stagnation, sales_win_dna, 
sales_win_probability. Plus the deals and stage_transition_logs tables and all sales routes.
Why: fewer than half of businesses use a CRM at all, and the features that justify the price disable when stage 
history is absent, which is the normal case for a CSV export. No local advantage. Highest remaining build cost, 
smallest market.
Do not leave it dormant. Dormant code still generates bugs, review burden and confusion about what the 
product is.
4.2 Ecommerce depth features — 13 services
Delete: ecommerce_rfm, ecommerce_rfm_endpoint, ecommerce_churn, ecommerce_holt_winters, 
ecommerce_inventory_forecast, ecommerce_sku_matrix, ecommerce_diagnostics, ecommerce_returns, 
ecommerce_ad_kill_switch, ecommerce_playbook, ecommerce_olist_adapter. Plus the 
rfm_segment_assignments, sku_inventory and ad_kill_audit_log tables.
Why: every one of these requires data a Nigerian retailer does not have — customer identity, stock counts, ad 
spend, returns records. Measured on real data: only 11 of 354 product strings had the 8 weeks of history Holt-
Winters requires. These features exist to serve a Shopify merchant, and that is not who you are selling to.
4.3 Bank features that do not serve a credit decision
Delete for now: bank_customer_segmentation (counterparty grouping — also the main channel through which 
confidential payee data leaks to loan officers), bank_revenue_patterns (seasonality requires 24 months, which 
almost no user will have).
These can return later. They are not wrong, they are simply not on the path to a signed lender.

4.4 Mothball, do not delete
payments.py, paystack_client.py, flutterwave_client.py, payment_provider.py, and the subscriptions and 
payment_transactions tables. Move to a branch and remove the routes from main. You are not charging SMEs at 
pilot and institutional invoicing happens offline. This code works; you just do not need it running, and every live 
endpoint is attack surface.
Also mothball: generated_reports and report_schedules, and the notification scheduling layer, until there is a 
customer asking for scheduled delivery.
5. BUILD — WHAT THE NEW PRD MUST ADD
None of these exist today. They are the difference between the old specification and a product a Nigerian lender 
will buy.
5.1 Identity
BVN capture and verification. There is no identity layer at all today. Open banking access is BVN-anchored, 
and without it you cannot link one business's several accounts into a single entity — which the interbank netting 
logic already assumes exists.
Business entity model, separate from the user account. One business, several bank accounts, one assessment.
5.2 Multi-account consolidation — move from post-MVP to core
Nigerian SMEs hold two to four accounts: a commercial bank for formality, a fintech wallet for daily trade, 
sometimes a cooperative. Analysing one account systematically understates the business. This is not an 
enhancement, it is a correctness requirement for this market.
5.3 Cash-gap reconciliation — the feature nobody else has
Compare bank inflows against uploaded sales records. Report the difference as verified unbanked cash revenue. 
Example from real data: bank inflows 5,200,000; sales records 8,633,800; unbanked cash revenue 3,433,800, or 
40 percent.
This is the entire reason the ecommerce ingestion survives. For a lender assessing a trader whose bank statement 
looks thin, this single number converts a decline into an approval. Specify it as a first-class output with its own 
confidence treatment, not as a footnote.
5.4 Consent, retention and audit — NDPA obligations
Stored consent records with timestamp, scope and revocation. A documented retention period per data class. A 
working delete-my-data endpoint. An access audit log recording who viewed which assessment and when. No 
institution will send you a real customer statement without these, and NDPC registration assumes they exist.
5.5 The verifiable assessment link
Replace the lender brief PDF with a shareable link that shows the analysis, the source, the ingestion date and the 
integrity check result. A PDF can be edited. A link backed by the reconciliation record cannot. This is where the 
attestation asset becomes visible to the buyer.
5.6 Outcome tracking
Log every score issued and record the repayment result when it becomes known. In eighteen months an 
accuracy claim backed by real outcome data is the only asset a foundation model cannot replicate. Start 
collecting before you have anything to prove.

5.7 Currency reality
Report revenue trends in both nominal and real terms. A 12-month NGN trend during devaluation shows growth 
that is not real, and a lender reading 'improving trajectory' off inflation is being misled.

6.4 New tables the PRD must define
business_entities — the legal business, with BVN, CAC number and owner. Parent of several accounts.
consent_records — subject, scope, granted_at, revoked_at, source (Mono, open banking, direct upload).
data_access_log — who accessed which assessment, when, from where.
assessments — the unit of value and the unit of billing. Replaces the loose notion of an analysis run as the thing 
an institution pays for.
assessment_shares — the verifiable link, its recipient, expiry and view history.
assessment_outcomes — loan approved or declined, amount, repayment status, for accuracy measurement.
6.5 Redesign required
user_merchant_roles — the current role sets are generic owner, admin, manager, viewer. The PRD defines 
semantic roles with distinct scopes: Account Owner or CFO, Accountant, Loan Officer, Analyst read-only. The 
generic model cannot express 'loan officer sees the brief but not transaction detail', which is why that defect 
exists. Redesign the role model before rewriting the RBAC matrix.

8. RULES FOR WRITING THE NEW PRD
1. One document, not four. Three PRDs plus a Developer Guide plus a Mapping Guide produced three 
contradictory net margin formulas and a k=6 clustering spec with seven labels. One specification, versioned, 
with a change log.
2. Specify the security and compliance layer. The previous specification's entire security section was two table 
rows. Password policy, token lifetime, rate limits, file validation, encryption scope, consent, retention, audit, 
RBAC matrix — all of it belongs in the PRD, because everything absent from the PRD became a critical defect 
in the code.
3. Define a data maturity ladder. Mono connected, statement uploaded, statement plus sales records, multi-
account. State exactly what each rung unlocks and what stays disabled. This replaces the assumption that every 
user has the same data.
4. Never label a number with a word the data cannot support. Gross profit, contribution margin and net profit are 
three different things. Show only the one the inputs justify. The old specification's net margin label collapsed to 
gross profit for any business without shipping, ad spend and processing fees — which is every physical retailer.
5. Write the accuracy targets so they can be tested. 'False positive rate below 5 percent' against no labelled 
dataset is not a Definition of Done, it is a wish. State the measurement method or remove the target.
6. Write it after the interviews, not before. Ten conversations using the six questions, then the PRD. The 
specification should be written by your buyers.
